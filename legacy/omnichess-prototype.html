OmniChess: 2P to 6P Geometry Guide

1. Unified Radial Sector Topology (2 to 6 Players)

Each player commands an 8×4 sector (32 squares) with a full 16-piece chess army:

  • 2-Player (64 cells): Classic 8×8 square chess board.
  • 3-Player (96 cells): Hexagonal arena with 3 sectors meeting at a 6-cell central rosette.
  • 4-Player (128 cells): Octagonal arena with 4 sectors meeting at an 8-cell rosette.
  • 5-Player (160 cells): Decagonal arena with 5 sectors meeting at a 10-cell rosette.
  • 6-Player (192 cells): Dodecagonal arena with 6 sectors meeting at a 12-cell rosette.

2. Orthogonal & Diagonal Seam Transitions

Rooks & Queens: Left-wing files (a–d) cross into the adjacent clockwise sector; right-wing files (e–h) cross into the counter-clockwise sector. Bishops & Queens: Crossing the central vertex allows diagonals to branch into any opposing sector!

3. Spectator Mode & Seat Controls

Click Spectate to watch an all-AI battle with Pause, Step, and Speed controls. You can also click any player's HUMAN / AI badge on their left status card to take over or delegate any army mid-match!

4. 3P+ King's Conquest (Squad Conversion)

When Conquest: ON is active, checkmating or capturing an enemy King immediately converts all of that player's remaining pieces on the board into the killer's color and command!

5. Estimated Win Chances

Army cards compare material, available movement, center control, pawn progress, threatened pieces, and king safety after each move. Active armies share 100%; eliminated armies have 0%. These are heuristic estimates, not measured probabilities, and do not predict draws. Conquest is reflected through the converted army's new position and material.
